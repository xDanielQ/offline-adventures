You are designing a controlled activity catalog for an open-source project called Offline Adventures.

PROJECT PURPOSE

Offline Adventures is a local, privacy-first application for the Hacktoberfest 2026 "Touch Grass" challenge.

The application should:
- reduce decision fatigue;
- encourage people to engage with their immediate surroundings;
- prefer outdoor activities when conditions are suitable;
- provide sheltered or indoor bridge activities during unsuitable weather;
- create opportunities to record small local memories;
- avoid exact locations, GPS, live weather, cloud services, and personal data.

TASK

Create exactly 50 genuinely different activities.

Required distribution:
- exactly 30 outdoor activities;
- exactly 8 sheltered activities;
- exactly 12 indoor_bridge activities.

Indoor bridge activities must connect to:
- a previous local experience;
- observation through a window or sheltered place;
- preparation for a future outdoor activity;
- creation or organization of local memories.

Do not pad the catalog with superficial variants.
Two activities are duplicates if they have essentially the same goal and only change a color, season, object, duration, participant, or equipment item.

SAFETY BOUNDARIES

Do not include:
- wild-food foraging;
- tasting unknown plants or objects;
- entering private or restricted property;
- isolated nighttime exploration;
- swimming, boating, kayaking, climbing, or dangerous terrain;
- medical or fitness advice;
- wildlife handling or feeding;
- collecting protected plants;
- photographing identifiable people without permission;
- activities that require exact addresses or GPS;
- activities that assume current weather conditions;
- activities that require specialist knowledge for safe completion.

Use neutral safety language.
Do not claim that an activity is completely safe.
Do not invent local places, routes, landmarks, opening hours, or distances.

ALLOWED ENUM VALUES

environment:
- outdoor
- sheltered
- indoor_bridge

energy_levels:
- low
- medium
- high

weather:
- dry
- light_rain
- heavy_rain
- strong_wind
- snow
- very_hot
- very_cold
- unknown

IDENTIFIERS

Use identifiers from activity_001 through activity_050.
Each identifier and name must be unique.

OUTPUT SCHEMA

Return one JSON array only.
Do not use Markdown.
Do not include an introduction, conclusion, explanation, or summary.

Each array element must match this structure:

{
  "id": "activity_001",
  "name": "Short unique English name",
  "environment": "outdoor",
  "minimum_minutes": 20,
  "maximum_minutes": 45,
  "energy_levels": [
    "low",
    "medium"
  ],
  "weather": [
    "dry",
    "light_rain"
  ],
  "interests": [
    "nature",
    "photography",
    "observation"
  ],
  "requires_equipment": [
    "phone_or_camera"
  ],
  "mission_steps": [
    "First concrete step.",
    "Second concrete step.",
    "Third concrete step."
  ],
  "safety_notes": [
    "One specific and proportionate safety boundary."
  ],
  "memory_prompts": [
    "One question that helps preserve a factual memory."
  ]
}

VALIDATION RULES

- minimum_minutes must be between 5 and 240;
- maximum_minutes must be between 5 and 240;
- minimum_minutes cannot exceed maximum_minutes;
- energy_levels must contain at least one allowed value;
- weather must contain at least one allowed value;
- interests must contain between 1 and 5 short lowercase tags;
- requires_equipment may be empty;
- mission_steps must contain between 1 and 5 concrete instructions;
- safety_notes must contain at least one item;
- memory_prompts must contain between 1 and 3 questions;
- all user-facing text must be in English;
- the JSON must be valid as a complete document.

QUALITY CHECK BEFORE RETURNING

Before returning the JSON:
1. Count the records and verify there are exactly 50.
2. Verify the distribution is 30 outdoor, 8 sheltered, and 12 indoor_bridge.
3. Verify there are no duplicate IDs.
4. Verify there are no duplicate names.
5. Remove semantically duplicated activities.
6. Verify every value uses the allowed enums.
7. Verify every activity has a clear link to local surroundings or local memories.
8. Verify the response contains only the JSON array.
